---
name: Legal rule authority
description: How procurement references should become executable compliance rules.
---

Compliance rules must record their source, authority level, jurisdiction or organization, effective dates, and version. Official regulations outrank socialization decks, commentary, and operational analysis.

**Why:** The Perpres 46/2025 reference set includes official regulations, presentation materials, local examples, and secondary analysis. Several village procurement thresholds and implementation claims differ or are illustrative rather than nationally binding.

**How to apply:** Do not hard-code thresholds, sanctions, formulas, or BUMN/BUMD applicability from slides. Verify the official text, model local rules as versioned configuration, and preserve the legal basis used for every compliance decision.
